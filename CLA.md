# Contributor Licence Agreement

> **Draft for review.** Written by the maintainer for the first public release and not yet
> reviewed by a lawyer. It follows the shape of the Apache Software Foundation's individual
> CLA, cut down to what a one-person project needs: the right to keep relicensing the
> platform (for example to move a module into the closed brain, or to offer it to an acquirer)
> without going back to every contributor.

Thank you for your interest in the Cophyla project ("the Project"), maintained by its
author ("we"). This agreement records the terms under which you contribute. It covers every
contribution you submit from the day you agree, until you tell us in writing that you
withdraw for future contributions; it never applies retroactively to a contribution you
did not make.

## 1. Definitions

"You" means the individual or entity agreeing here. "Contribution" means any original work
of authorship, including modifications or additions to existing work, that you intentionally
submit to the Project for inclusion in it — code, tests, documentation, fixtures, views. A
work you mark as "not a contribution" in writing is not one.

You are legally entitled to grant these licences. If your employer has rights to

say otherwise in writing it is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.

## 5. Notice

You will tell us if any of the above becomes inaccurate.

## How to agree

Add the line `Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>` to the commit message of every
commit in your pull request (`git commit -s`), or say in the pull request that you agree to
CLA.md as of that date. The first pull request from a new contributor should say so
explicitly. We keep the list of who agreed in the repository's history.
